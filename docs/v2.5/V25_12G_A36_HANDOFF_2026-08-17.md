## 5. Why `±0.5/R` Does Not Relax the Turning Radius

For the canonical verified minimum turning radius:

```text
R = 1.50 m
```

existing saturated primitive:

```text
|kappa| = 1/R = 0.666666... 1/m
turning radius = 1.50 m
```

new intermediate primitive:

```text
|kappa| = 0.5/R = 0.333333... 1/m
turning radius = 3.00 m
```

Therefore the added primitives are less curved than the existing saturated primitive.

Hard invariant:

```text
abs(kappa) <= 1/R
```

for every generated primitive.
