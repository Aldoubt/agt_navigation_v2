Definitions:

```text

= a sampled candidate footprint touches/crosses Site Boundary

limited sample
= at that longitudinal station at least one candidate is Navigation-Grid FREE
```

The corrected result is important:


## Planned V25-12G boundary

V25-12G will not require full mandatory aisle coverage.

Its planning unit will be vehicle-feasible aisle segments rather than immutable whole aisles.

The objective will allow:

```text
skip infeasible segments with explicit reasons
use forward motion where possible
use bounded reverse motion where useful
connect feasible segments under Site Boundary / footprint / kinematic constraints
maximize useful traversable coverage rather than force every structural aisle to succeed
```

V25-12G remains a separate design increment and is not implemented by V25-12F.
