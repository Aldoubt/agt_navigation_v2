## 10. What A3.5 establishes

A3.5 establishes all of the following for the frozen real dataset and diagnostic HEAD:

1. The A3 behavior result is stable: **0/40 inter-segment transitions are executable**.
2. The diagnostic instrumentation does not alter the pre-A3.5 behavior projection.
3. All 40 R6B searches terminate by queue exhaustion rather than expansion-budget exhaustion.
4. Raising `max_expansions` above 30,000 is not supported by the observed failure mechanism.
5. A single universal Site Boundary explanation is insufficient: only 2/40 connectors classify as `SITE_BOUNDARY_LIMITED`.
6. A single universal Navigation Grid/footprint explanation is also insufficient: only 3/40 classify as `FOOTPRINT_GRID_LIMITED`.
7. The majority, 26/40, show multiple simultaneously material limitations under the frozen classifier.
8. The failure distribution is highly side-dependent: HIGH_U accounts for 36/40 connectors and every mixed/inconclusive/goal-connection class.
9. Some failed searches reach positions very close to the goal, so raw position proximity alone is not equivalent to connector executability.

## 11. What A3.5 does not establish

A3.5 does not establish:

- that the 40 connectors are globally infeasible;
- that a different bounded local backend could not connect them;
- that Site Boundary, Navigation Grid, footprint or the 1.5 m Ackermann radius should be relaxed;
- that state discretization should be refined;
- that max path length, cusp count, search envelope or goal tolerances should be increased;
- that a Reeds-Shepp or other analytic backend is required;
- that A4 may proceed.

Those are design questions for a separate, explicitly approved backend-amendment phase.
