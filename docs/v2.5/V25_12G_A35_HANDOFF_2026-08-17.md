## 5. What the A3 Amendment Established

The old interpretation:

```text
all local forward Dubins candidates cross Site Boundary
=> whole connector hard rejected
```

was too strong.

Current semantics are frozen as:

```text
CONNECTOR_ENDPOINT_SITE_BOUNDARY_CONFLICT
```

for an illegal start/goal footprint. This is a hard rejection and must never enter R6B.

```text
LOCAL_FORWARD_PATH_SITE_BOUNDARY_CONFLICT
```

means both endpoint footprints are legal but the locally relevant forward-only Dubins family crosses the hard Site Boundary. This class may enter R6B because R6B independently re-checks the Site Boundary for every preview footprint.

Real data showed all 33 old broad boundary conflicts were the second class, not endpoint conflicts.

## 8. Mandatory A4 Entry Gate

A4 remains blocked until the real greenhouse graph contains a continuous service chain:

```text
segment A
 -> EXECUTABLE transition 1
segment B
 -> EXECUTABLE transition 2
segment C
```

Mandatory counts:

```text
>= 3 distinct vehicle-feasible segment_ids
>= 2 distinct aisle_ids
>= 2 EXECUTABLE inter-segment transitions
```

The three segments must span at least two different aisles. Three segments from only one aisle are not accepted.

A stronger preference, not a hard gate: at least one transition should be a normal cross-aisle headland maneuver rather than connectivity achieved only by an unusually long reverse maneuver.
