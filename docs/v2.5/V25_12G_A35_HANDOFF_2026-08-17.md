## 8. A4 Entry Gate

A4 Maximum Feasible Coverage remains blocked until the real graph contains one continuous service chain:

```text

 -> EXECUTABLE transition 1
segment B
 -> EXECUTABLE transition 2
segment C
```

with:

```text
>= 3 distinct vehicle-feasible segment_ids
>= 2 distinct aisle_ids
>= 2 EXECUTABLE inter-segment transitions
```

The three segments must span at least two aisles.

A stronger preference, not a hard gate: at least one recovered transition should be a normal cross-aisle headland maneuver rather than connectivity obtained only through an unusually long reverse path.

## 9. Next Design Stage

A3.5 is complete and frozen. The next step is a separately approved **minimal backend amendment**.

A3.5 evidence rules out expansion-budget tuning as the first response and does not support weakening safety/map constraints. Candidate amendment families remain:


A full Hybrid-A* replacement is intentionally not the default next step.

The next design must choose one falsifiable hypothesis and change only the smallest behavior surface needed to test it. A negative real-data result is acceptable evidence and must be frozen rather than hidden.
